# Map: Safe-zones

Status: ready-for-human
Cross-ref: [GH #38](https://github.com/assofohdz/subspace-infinity/issues/38)
Labels: area:server, backlog

Implement fly-over safe zones (see the map upgrade work item).

From Wikipedia:
> Safe zones allow players to evade enemies or simply take a break from the game. While in a safe zone, a ship cannot take damage but also cannot fire. Most safe zones have a time limit; if a player stays in a safe zone too long, he is kicked from the game and must rejoin the zone. This prevents players from camping out in a safe zone and leaving their computer idle for an extended period of time in order to maintain their state in the game.
